🎯 Positioning

Core Message

"Real human-performed instruments. No AI slop. Licensed & legal."


Musio owns the anti-AI, pro-human-creator angle. The market is swinging away from AI music (iHeartRadio won't play it, "Say No To Suno" movement emerging). We can own "the authentic alternative."

Key Differentiators

  • Real human performance — AI can't replicate this
  • Fully licensed — no copyright lawsuits, no legal risk
  • Cloud-based — no manual installs
  • Ultra-light CPU footprint
  • $20/month for everything — vs $4,500+ in individual libraries
